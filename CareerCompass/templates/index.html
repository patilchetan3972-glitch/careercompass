The Challenge & Our Solution

Why Engineering Students Need CareerCompass

Many students struggle with career confusion, an overload of fragmented tutorials, and a lack of self-assessment regarding industry readiness.

Career Confusion

Students are overwhelmed by shifting industry buzzwords and struggle to know which specialization fits their natural problem-solving strengths.

Random Course Selection

Students enroll in disconnected online tutorials without understanding prerequisite dependencies or whether the topics fill an actual skill gap.

Objective Self-Assessment

Our 25-MCQ diagnostic evaluates practical logic, foundational knowledge, and problem-solving without timer pressure or negative marking.

Structured 3-Stage Roadmaps

We connect you to reputable, 100% free learning resources (Kaggle Learn, freeCodeCamp, W3Schools, SQLBolt) with persistent task tracking.

UN Global Goals Alignment

Contributing to Sustainable Development

Developed by second-year AI & Data Science engineering students, CareerCompass directly advances United Nations Sustainable Development Goals 4 and 8.

SDG 4: Quality Education

Inclusive & Equitable Technical Education

Target 4.4: Substantially increase the number of youth with relevant technical and vocational skills for employment and entrepreneurship.

CareerCompass demystifies technical skills by providing free, individualized career diagnosis and curated roadmaps. By guiding students to free, high-quality educational resources, it levels the playing field for learners regardless of economic background.

SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth

Sustained Employability & Youth Opportunity

Target 8.6: Substantially reduce the proportion of youth not in employment, education or training (NEET).

By aligning college learning directly with industry-required skills across AI, Data Science, and Web Engineering, CareerCompass prevents unemployment resulting from qualification-skill mismatches and accelerates job readiness.
